How the ranking works

  1. Category multiple: median mcap/TVL of tokened protocols in the same category, winsorized to the global [p25, p75] and shrunk toward the global median with a prior of 5 — so a category with 1 comparable cannot dominate the board.
  2. Size: min(1, (100M/TVL)^0.12) — smaller protocols are discounted.
  3. Growth is RELATIVE: a protocol's 7d change minus its category median, clamped to [-50, 100], scaled by 0.4 — so a protocol is judged against its own category, not the market.
  4. Maturity: 0.65 + min(age, 1095)/1095 × 0.35 — older protocols without a token score higher.

Implied value = TVL × multiple × size × growth × maturity. Every number comes from the DefiLlama snapshot; no protocol name is hardcoded.

The board covers the 40 watchlist protocols plus the 160 largest other tokenless protocols, excluding exchange and bridge products, staking wrappers, basis-trading and prediction-market products, managed curator vaults, and versioned (V2/V3) forks.
